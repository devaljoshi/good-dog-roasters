The Giving Ledger

Made in a Florida cottage food operation

"Made in a cottage food operation that is not subject to Florida's food safety regulations."

Artisan coffee is roasted by hand in our Palm Beach County home kitchen under Florida's Cottage Food Law (Florida Statute 500.80). That law lets us sell directly to you, but only within Florida — so Artisan bags ship to Florida addresses only. Every bag carries the statement above. Our Everyday Collection is roasted by licensed commercial roasters and ships nationwide. Shipping details →
